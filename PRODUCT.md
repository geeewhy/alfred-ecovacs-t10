# HQ

## Register

product

## Users

The owner and developer of an extended Ecovacs T10 Omni. HQ is used on the local network to inspect the robot deeply, confirm that custom services are healthy, and eventually drive it directly.

## Product Purpose

HQ is the human interface to Alfred. Its Node server is both a web server for the browser and a trusted local client of the robot's authenticated interfaces. Home answers whether the robot is reachable and healthy. Cockpit will become the focused remote-control workspace.

## Brand Personality

Precise, quiet, technical.

## Anti-references

No flashy smart-home dashboard, decorative gradients, oversized metric cards, glass effects, or dense wall of widgets. Avoid Apple-like lifestyle presentation. Linear is the closer reference: disciplined hierarchy and compact product density.

## Design Principles

- Put connection truth first. Never imply a stale robot is online.
- Prefer aligned technical detail over decorative visualization.
- Keep controls calm until the user's intent makes them active.
- Expose the real system architecture and verified values.
- Grow Cockpit from discovered capabilities instead of invented controls.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use sound semantic HTML and readable contrast as defaults. Deeper keyboard and assistive-technology work will proceed incrementally with the product.
